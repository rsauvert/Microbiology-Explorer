Analytical Gel Electrophoresis

⚡ Gel Electrophoresis Lab

Agarose and polyacrylamide gels for nucleic acids and proteins. Pour the gel, load the wells, connect the power supply, run, stain, and interpret the bands.

The principle in one sentence

Charged molecules placed in a porous gel and an electric field move toward the electrode of opposite charge. The gel acts as a molecular sieve, so small molecules travel farther than large ones.

Charge
Nucleic acids carry one negative phosphate per nucleotide. In SDS-PAGE, SDS gives proteins a uniform negative coat. Either way the charge-to-mass ratio is roughly constant.
Sieving matrix
Agarose (large pores) or polyacrylamide (small, tunable pores). A higher gel % gives smaller pores and better resolution of small molecules.
Readout
In the linear range, migration distance falls linearly with log₁₀(size). A ladder of known sizes turns distance into bp, nt, or kDa.

Nucleic acids: what changes migration

  • Length: the main variable. Distance is proportional to −log₁₀(bp).
  • Conformation: an uncut plasmid runs as supercoiled (fastest), linear, and nicked/open-circular (slowest).
  • Gel %: low % for large fragments, high % for small ones.
  • Voltage: too high heats the gel, causing "smiling" lanes, smeared bands, and even melted agarose.
  • RNA: secondary structure distorts migration; denaturing gels (formaldehyde agarose, urea-PAGE) give true sizes.

Proteins: SDS-PAGE essentials

  • SDS binds ~1.4 g per g of protein, unfolds it, and masks its intrinsic charge, so separation is by mass.
  • Reducing agent breaks disulfide bonds. IgG runs at ~150 kDa unreduced, or as 50 + 25 kDa chains when reduced.
  • Stacking gel concentrates samples into a thin starting zone (Laemmli discontinuous system).
  • Native PAGE omits SDS: proteins separate by charge, size, and shape and keep their activity.
  • Afterwards, proteins can be transferred to a membrane for a Western blot.

Microbiology applications

PCR detection
Virulence or resistance genes (such as mecA, toxin genes): one band at the expected size, read against positive and no-template controls.
Plasmid analysis
Plasmid profiles, restriction mapping, and checking a cloning step.
Outbreak typing
PFGE of rare-cutter genomic digests was the reference method for decades. Whole-genome sequencing has now largely replaced it.
Protein profiles
Whole-cell and outer-membrane protein patterns, recombinant protein purification, toxin subunits.
Serology
Western blots show which antigen bands a patient's antibodies recognize.
Safety
EtBr is a mutagen, so prefer SYBR Safe or GelRed. Unpolymerized acrylamide is a neurotoxin. Shield your eyes and skin from UV. The power supply carries high voltage.

How to use: Gel Electrophoresis Lab

In brief: Run agarose, native PAGE and SDS-PAGE gels for nucleic acids and proteins, size unknown bands from a standard curve, and interpret real-world gels.

Gel Bench: step by step

  1. Cast: choose a gel system (agarose, native PAGE or SDS-PAGE) and a percentage.
  2. Prep: for proteins, decide whether to heat with SDS and add a reducing agent.
  3. Load: click a tube in the rack, then click a well on the gel (or press Demo load).
  4. Connect: choose which electrode sits at the wells. Remember: Run to Red (+).
  5. Run: set the voltage and time, then press Run gel and watch the tracking dyes.
  6. Stain: choose a stain and press Stain & image. Click any band for its identity and size, and read the Run report.
